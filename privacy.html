Settle: Baby Names

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information — and the few services we rely on to run the app.

Last updated 25 September 2026

Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Settle: Baby Names ("Settle", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the app. Settle: Baby Names is published by Dave App Development Ltd (trading as Settle) and distributed under the package identifier com.settlebabynames.

By using Settle you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to run the app:

  • Account identifiers from Google Sign-In: your email address and your Google account ID. Signing in requests the basic "profile" and "email" scopes, but we store only your email and Google account ID. Your name and profile picture are discarded and never saved.
  • Your activity in the app: the names you swipe on (approve, reject, or superlike), your filter preferences (gender, decade, and origin), any custom names you add, and your saved match rankings.
  • Your couple link: if you pair with a partner, we store the connection between your two accounts so that your shared names and matches can sync.
  • Sign-in sessions: a session credential for each device you sign in on. On Android it is held in the device's secure keystore; on our servers it is stored only as a one-way hash, never in its original form.
  • Subscription status: if you buy Premium, we store only whether your subscription is active and when it expires. We never receive or store your card or payment details — payment is handled entirely by the payment provider for where you bought (the app store on mobile, or Paddle on the web), as described under "Payments" below.
  • A small daily usage counter, used to enforce the free daily swipe limit.
  • Crash and stability data: in the Android and iPhone apps we use Google Firebase Crashlytics to record crashes and errors. When the app crashes or hits an error we want visibility on, Crashlytics collects a diagnostic report containing the technical details of the failure (such as the error and the stack trace), together with information about the app and device at the time (for example app version, operating-system version, device model, and a Crashlytics-generated installation identifier). We use these reports only to find and fix bugs. They are not used for advertising, and the web version of Settle sends no crash reports.
  • Analytics and advertising-measurement data: through Google Firebase Analytics we collect standard app-usage and device information (such as app version, device model, and general region), a Google advertising identifier where available (on iOS only if you allow it, see "Analytics and advertising measurement" below), and, when you first buy Premium, a purchase event recording the amount, currency, and store transaction identifier.

How we use your information

We use your information only to operate the app:

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To store your preferences and the names you swipe on.
  • To sync names and matches between you and your partner.
  • To enforce the free daily swipe limit and to unlock unlimited swiping for Premium subscribers.
  • To understand how the app is used and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising, using Google Firebase Analytics (see below).
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and errors, using Google Firebase Crashlytics (see "Crash reporting" below).

We do not sell your data. We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand usage and to measure our advertising, and Google Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes.

Analytics and advertising measurement

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. We do not sell your data.

When you first purchase Premium, we send Google Firebase Analytics a "purchase" event recording the purchase amount, its currency, and the store transaction identifier. This is linked to Google Ads so that, if you arrived from one of our advertisements, the purchase can be attributed to it, helping us reach more people who would enjoy Settle. This event is sent only on an initial purchase, never on a renewal or a restore.

Firebase Analytics also collects standard, largely aggregated information about how the app is used and about your device (for example app version, device model, and general region), together with a Google advertising identifier where available. On iOS this advertising identifier (the IDFA) is used only if you allow it through the App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, the app works in exactly the same way and the purchase event is still recorded, only without the advertising identifier. You can change your tracking choice at any time in your device's Settings.

In the Android and iPhone apps, when you sign in we also give RevenueCat (our subscription provider, see "Third-party services") the identifier Firebase Analytics assigns to your installation of the app. It is not your name or email address. If you then buy Premium after being referred by a friend, our server uses it to record that purchase in Google Firebase Analytics against your app installation, so we can measure how well referrals work. It is not used for advertising.

Crash reporting

The Android and iPhone apps use Google Firebase Crashlytics so that when Settle crashes we can see what went wrong and fix it. Without it a crash would leave no trace and we would have no way of knowing it happened.

When the app crashes, or hits an error we have asked to be told about, Crashlytics sends Google a diagnostic report on our behalf. It contains the technical details of the failure (such as the error and the stack trace showing where in the code it happened) along with the state of the app and device at the time: app version, operating-system version, device model and settings, and an installation identifier that Crashlytics generates for your app install. That identifier is reset if you delete and reinstall the app.

We use crash reports only to diagnose and fix problems in the app. They are never used for advertising, and we do not use them to build a profile of you. Crash reporting is part of the native apps only — the web version of Settle at settledevelopment.com sends no crash reports.

Payments

Settle offers an optional paid Premium upgrade. We never see or store your card or full payment details. Payment is processed entirely by a payment provider that acts as the merchant of record for your purchase, which differs by where you buy:

  • On the web (settledevelopment.com), payments are processed by Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd), our authorised reseller and merchant of record. To take payment and meet its tax and legal obligations, Paddle collects information such as your name, email address, billing address, and payment details directly, and handles billing, invoicing, sales tax/VAT, and refunds. Paddle acts as an independent data controller for that payment information; its handling is governed by Paddle's buyer privacy notice at paddle.com/legal/privacy.
  • In the Android app, payments are processed by Google Play; in the iPhone or iPad app (coming soon), by Apple. Their handling of your payment information is governed by Google's and Apple's own privacy policies.

In every case we receive only a confirmation of your subscription status — whether Premium is active and when it expires — never your card number or full payment details.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Cloud (Cloud Run) and in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon. All communication between the app and our servers uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf; we do not sell or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

  • Google: for Google Sign-In, for hosting our backend on Google Cloud, (through Google Firebase Analytics and Google Ads) for app analytics and measuring the effectiveness of our advertising (see "Analytics and advertising measurement"), and (through Google Firebase Crashlytics, in the mobile apps only) for crash and error reporting (see "Crash reporting").
  • Neon: for hosting our PostgreSQL database.
  • RevenueCat, and the payment provider for where you buy — the app store (Google Play or the App Store) for in-app purchases, or Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd) for purchases made on the web: only if you purchase Premium, to process the payment and to manage your subscription status. Payment is handled by that provider as merchant of record (see "Payments" above); we never receive your card details.
  • RevenueCat also holds, in the mobile apps and whether or not you purchase, your app installation's Firebase Analytics identifier, so that a referred purchase can be recorded in analytics (see "Analytics and advertising measurement").

The only analytics we use is Google Firebase Analytics, and the only crash reporting we use is Google Firebase Crashlytics, both as described above.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active.

You can permanently delete your account and its data at any time from inside the app: open Settings (the gear icon in the top-right header), scroll to Delete account, then confirm by typing DELETE. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

When you delete your account we remove your account record, your sign-in sessions, and your swipes. If you are not linked with a partner, we also delete your custom names, your match rankings, and your couple record. If you are linked with a partner, your partner keeps their own account and the custom names you added together. These are shared by the couple and have no separate per-person ownership.

Live data is deleted immediately when you confirm. Copies may remain in routine encrypted backups for up to 24 hours before those backups are rotated out, after which no copy remains.

Children

Settle is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact
