Settle: my Baby

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect the information you record about your baby — and the few services we rely on to run the app.

Last updated 25 July 2026

Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Settle: my Baby ("Settle", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the app. Settle: my Baby is published by Dave App Development Ltd (trading as Settle) and distributed under the package identifier com.settlebaby.

Settle: my Baby is a baby-tracking app for parents and carers. You use it to log and review your baby's feeding, sleep, nappies, growth and related care. By using the app you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to run the app:

  • Account identifiers. When you create an account we store an account identifier and the email address you sign in with, through Firebase Authentication (Google). This lets you sign in and sync across your devices.
  • Your household and baby profile. The household and caregivers you set up, each caregiver's role and permissions, and each baby's details — name, date of birth, birth sex, and (if you provide it) due date, which the app uses to show corrected age.
  • The care you log. The events you record about your baby — for example feeds, sleep, nappies, growth measurements, pumping, and any notes or clinical details you choose to enter. Some of this is health-related information about your child. You decide what to record; the app never adds health information on its own.
  • Voice logging (if you use it). When you log by voice, your speech is turned into text on your device. Only the resulting text is sent to our AI parsing service to convert it into a structured entry — the audio recording is not uploaded to or stored on our servers.
  • Subscription status. If you buy a subscription, we store whether your household's subscription is active and its renewal date, through RevenueCat and the app stores. We never receive or store your card or payment details.
  • Device and diagnostic data. Basic technical data needed to keep the app working, secure, and to understand how it is used (see "Analytics" below).

How we use your information

  • To run the core app — record your entries and keep them in sync in real time across your devices and the caregivers in your household.
  • To provide the app's reflection features, which compare your baby's own logged data against published, age-based guidance. The app describes and normalises — it does not diagnose, predict, or give medical advice.
  • To turn your voice notes into structured entries when you use voice logging.
  • To manage your subscription and the features it unlocks.
  • To keep the app secure, diagnose problems, and understand how it is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use the information you record about your baby to serve you advertising.

Services we rely on

To run the app we share the minimum necessary information with a small number of processors, each acting on our instructions:

  • Google (Firebase & Google Cloud). Firebase Authentication for sign-in, and Cloud Firestore and Google Cloud for real-time sync and hosting our backend.
  • Neon. Our primary database (PostgreSQL), where your entries are stored as the authoritative record. Neon runs on cloud infrastructure in the United States.
  • Google Cloud (Vertex AI). When you use voice logging, the text transcript is processed by a large language model hosted on Google Cloud's Vertex AI to convert it into a structured entry. The transcript is used only to produce that entry.
  • RevenueCat and the app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play). To manage subscriptions and confirm entitlement.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Firebase (both provided by Google) to understand how the app is used — for example which features are opened and whether the app is working reliably — so that we can improve it. This uses usage and device data and identifiers assigned by these services; it does not include the health information you record about your baby. We do not sell this data or use it to serve you advertising.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored and processed on the services listed above, which operate in the United States and elsewhere. Where your data is transferred outside your country, we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers. Data in transit and at rest is protected using industry-standard encryption.

Your data, and your rights

You are in control of the information you record:

  • Access and export. You can export your baby's records from the app at any time, as a readable summary and as a raw data file, through your device's normal share options. Exports are generated on your device and are not stored on our servers.
  • Correction. You can edit or remove any entry you have made.
  • Deletion. You can delete your account and its data from within the app, or by emailing us. Live data is removed when you confirm; copies may remain in routine encrypted backups for a short period before those backups are rotated out.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data (such as access, correction, deletion, and objection). To exercise them, contact us using the details below.

Children

Settle: my Baby is intended for use by adults — parents and carers — to record information about their own children. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly allow children to create accounts. Information about a baby is entered by the adult account holder, who is responsible for it, and we treat it as sensitive.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at alex@settledevelopment.com.
